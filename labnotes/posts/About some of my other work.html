Just wanted to let you folks know that over on OverTake.gg and GTPlanet.net, there's other tools i've built that complement your virtual driving. There's the Final Drive Lap Board, which is an obvious wink to the popular pokey british motoring show's way of logging hotlaps around their top secret test track just off the M5. Supports multiple sims and allows exporting lap times/boards to easy to process into other apps CSV files. The other one is a simple LCD-screen style SimHub Dash Studio Dashboard that shows your system properties like CPU,GPU and RAM temps and loads, along with uptime and current date/time.
