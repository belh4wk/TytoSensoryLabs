A focused photo-finishing workflow

Finish the picture.
Not your patience.

Off-Center Photo Finish is built for the part between choosing a photo and being happy enough to share it. Import a folder, open a picture, make the changes that matter, compare each editing layer, and export safe copies without wrestling with a full traditional editing suite.

It is intentionally focused rather than sprawling: powerful enough to be genuinely useful, direct enough to remain approachable, and structured around a radial editor that keeps the photograph—not a wall of controls—at the centre of the workflow.

The workflow

Three screens. One clear route.

1

Select

Choose individual photos, folders, or supported archives. Mark the pictures you want to work on and hold one to move straight into the editor.

2

Imagine

Edit in a darkroom-style workspace. The hollow radial menu keeps related tools grouped without permanently covering the image.

3

Export

Render new copies into a dated folder under Pictures. Originals remain untouched, and the completed output can be opened directly.

Editing tools

Enough control to finish the shot.

OCPF concentrates on the adjustments most people repeatedly need, then gives those tools a consistent interaction model instead of scattering them across unrelated panels.

Light and colour

Exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows, whites, blacks, temperature, tint, vibrance, saturation, and hue.

Frame and rotation

Fine or coarse rotation, crop and crop anchoring, with a touch-friendly dial on mobile.

Focus and atmosphere

Radial or tilt-shift focus treatments, vignette shaping, feathering, blur, and placement guides.

Colour wash and watermarking

Preset or custom colour washes plus light/dark watermark assets, positioning, sizing, and opacity control.

Touch-ups and cleanup

Brush local areas, adjust them independently, refine selections, and apply practical cleanup treatments without leaving the main workflow.

Layer-aware comparison

Compare Frame, Colour, Light, Focus, Touch-ups, Cleanup, and other categories without hiding unrelated edits that you still need for context.

Scope, honestly stated

Feature complete does not mean “every feature ever.”

“Feature complete” means the planned Off-Center Photo Finish workflow and its supported finishing tools are implemented for this release. It does not mean OCPF is trying to reproduce every capability, workflow, plug-in, asset-management system, or precision retouching tool found in a full professional photo suite.

What it is

A fast, purpose-built finishing application for selecting, improving, comparing, watermarking, and exporting photos with a low-friction interface.

What it is not

A replacement for every raw developer, cataloguing platform, compositing package, or pixel-level professional retouching workflow.

Questions

OCPF FAQ

Does OCPF overwrite my originals?
No. The normal export flow creates new finished copies in a dated Off-Center Photo Finish folder under Pictures. Your imported originals remain where they were.
Is this meant to replace Lightroom, Photoshop, Affinity Photo, or similar tools?
No. OCPF is intentionally narrower. It is for getting through a common finishing workflow quickly and intuitively, not for replacing every specialised feature of a full traditional suite.
Why a radial menu?
It keeps related controls close to the photograph and allows the same interaction language to work with a mouse or a finger. The menu can dim for comparison, then return to a clear editing state when you continue adjusting.
What does layer-aware Before/After mean?
When comparing Colour, for example, OCPF can hide only the Colour adjustments while leaving your rotation, framing, focus, and unrelated work visible. That makes the comparison useful instead of resetting the whole photograph every time.
Is Android fully supported?
The Android build is functional and included for testing, but the Windows version is the current primary support baseline. Phone hardware, Android versions, gallery providers, and manufacturer-specific behaviour can affect the mobile experience.
